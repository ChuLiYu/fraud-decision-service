# Presentation outline (draft)

Status: draft. Slide count, length, and language wait for the course format (scope open questions). Figures are generated by `make figures` into [`ml/reports/figures/`](../../ml/reports/figures/); numbers below come from the linked reports and must be re-read from them if the pipeline is rerun.

Story in one line: *a good fraud model is not enough; what matters is the decision it drives, under a review budget, real costs, and drift.*

Rubric mapping (ADR-004): creativity (slides 7–10), presentation (one message per slide, figures from code), comprehensiveness (slides 2–6), coding (slide 11).

## 1. Problem

- Card-not-present fraud, IEEE-CIS data: 590,540 transactions, 3.5% fraud, about 6 months ([dataset card](../data/dataset-card.md)).
- The question: approve, review, or decline each transaction, at the lowest cost.
- Notes: say early that all costs are scenario assumptions; no real bank data.

## 2. Method: a leakage-safe pipeline

- Stages with file handoff and manifests: fetch → prepare → train → evaluate → tune / fuse / decide → figures.
- Split by time (70 / 15 / 15); preprocessing fitted on train only; test used once per reported result.
- Notes: show the five leakage rules in the dataset card; each one has a test.

## 3. Feature engineering (time-safe)

- Per-card history features use only earlier transactions: prior count, prior mean amount, amount ratio, time since previous.
- Client key after the IEEE-CIS 1st place (card + address + card start day from `D1`), past-only means and distinct counts; the winners' look-ahead aggregation is not used.
- Figure: `progress.png`.
- Notes: per-card history added test PR-AUC +0.009 (within seed noise); the client key added +0.018 (0.562 → 0.580, run `m5-client`), mostly fewer false alarms and better ranking on product W.

## 4. Tuning, and how big noise is

- Grid on validation only; three seeds per candidate to measure noise.
- Figure: `seed_noise.png`.
- Notes: tuned setting is higher and steadier, but with three seeds the difference is not statistically conclusive ([tuning report](../../ml/reports/tuning/tuning.md)).

## 5. Models compared

- Logistic regression (baseline), MLP (comparison), LightGBM (main).
- Figure: `review_tradeoff.png`.
- Notes: trees win on hundreds of anonymous numeric columns; the MLP beats the linear baseline but not LightGBM ([comparison](../../ml/reports/comparison.md)).

## 6. Fusion ablation: a negative result

- Fit on early validation, choose on late validation, report on test; adoption rule fixed before the run.
- Figure: `fusion_diversity.png`.
- Notes: the models disagree (rank correlation 0.56 on test) but the MLP adds few unique catches, so fusion lost on test ([fusion report](../../ml/reports/fusion/fusion.md)).

## 7. From score to decision

- Three outcomes; costs per transaction in integer cents; review capacity as a constraint.
- Two policies: score thresholds, and expected cost (calibrated probability × amount).
- Notes: this is the creative angle; a model is evaluated as a decision system.

## 8. Decision cost

- Figure: `decision_cost.png`.
- Notes: same LightGBM model, different decision rule: the expected-cost policy saves a further $502–$661 per 1,000 transactions across capacities (bootstrap 95% intervals above zero) ([decision report](../../ml/reports/decision/decision.md)).

## 9. Does it depend on the cost assumptions?

- Figure: `sensitivity.png`.
- Notes: three scenarios, four capacities: the expected-cost policy is cheapest in all twelve.

## 10. Drift: what happens after deployment

- Figures: `time_drift.png`, `review_drift.png`.
- Notes: quality drops after the training period, and fixed policies send more than the planned share to review; thresholds need scheduled recalibration.

## 11. Engineering quality

- One command per stage; manifests with hashes, params, and commit; reports generated, never hand-edited.
- Tests: leakage (test labels flipped → nothing learned changes), saturation regression, manifest refusal; 64 tests.
- Notes: link the repository if publishing is allowed (scope open question).

## 12. Limitations and next steps

- Anonymized historical data; costs assumed; card fields are not verified identity.
- The expected-cost gain mixes two changes (calibration and amount); not yet separated.
- Next: serving the model behind a Java decision service ([scope phase 2](../product/scope.md#phase-2--synchronous-decision-service-s-2-to-s-5-s-10), after the course).
